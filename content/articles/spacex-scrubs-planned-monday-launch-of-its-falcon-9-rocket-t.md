The Starlink 10-19 mission is set to be SpaceX’s 72nd flight of its broadband internet satellites this year. Liftoff from pad 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station is scheduled for 10:26 a.m. EDT (1426 UTC) on Tuesday, Aug. 11.
